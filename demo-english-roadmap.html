Twelve arrested for allegedly running cat trafficking ring in Vietnam

Biên soạn từ nguồn BBC News · Cập nhật mới nhất

Twelve men have been arrested in Vietnam after allegedly confessing to stealing thousands of domestic cats and selling them to restaurants.

Police officers in the central province intercepted three transport vehicles loaded with wire cages containing over 300 feline animals earlier this week. Local authorities confirmed the animals were illegally transported across provincial borders without health certification.

Animal welfare organizations have voiced growing concerns over pet security and called for stricter legislative measures to eliminate commercial consumption of companion animals.
